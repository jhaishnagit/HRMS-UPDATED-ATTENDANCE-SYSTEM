Quality Policies & Objectives

As a leading provider of telecommunication services, we at Jhaishna Technologies are dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of quality in all aspects of our operations. This section outlines our Quality Policy, Company Objectives, and our commitment to deliver exceptional service to our customers.

Quality Policy

Our Quality Policy is built around the following key principles:

  • Customer-Centric Approach: We are committed to understanding and exceeding the evolving needs of our customers, ensuring our telecommunication services consistently meet or exceed their expectations.
  • Continuous Improvement: We embrace a culture of continuous improvement, constantly evaluating and refining our processes, technologies, and best practices to stay ahead of industry trends and deliver optimal performance.
  • Compliance and Regulatory Standards: We strictly adhere to all relevant industry regulations, standards, and legal requirements, ensuring that our operations maintain the highest levels of compliance and integrity.

Objectives

In line with our Quality Policy, we have set forth the following objectives to guide our teams and drive our success:

  • Automatic Service Activation Program: Our dedicated team ensures seamless activation and provisioning of telecommunication services, guaranteeing a smooth and efficient onboarding experience for our customers.
  • Billing and Revenue Management: Our teams in Operations, Testing, and Monitoring work tirelessly to ensure accurate and timely billing while proactively identifying and addressing any revenue-related issues.
  • OAP Design Development & Testing: We continuously improve our processes and deliver exceptional products/services. With a focus on Innovative Design, Seamless Development, Rigorous Testing, and Continuous Improvement, we aim to exceed customer expectations.

Staying at the forefront of innovation and delivering exceptional value to our customers is our main motto. Through our focus on design, development, and testing, we are confident in our ability to exceed your expectations and help you achieve your goals.

Company Policies

  • Attendance Policy: Employees must log in on time.
  • Shifts:
    • Morning Shift - 7:00AM to 4:00PM
    • General Shift - 10:00AM to 7:00PM
    • Second Shift - 1:00PM to 10:00PM
    • Night Shift - 10:00PM to 7:00AM
  • Leave Policy: 15 days annual leave allowed.
  • Remote Work: Allowed with prior approval.
  • Dress Code Policy:
    • Business casual attire is expected in the office at all times.
    • No Jeans, T-Shirts, Hoodies, or Shorts are allowed.
    • Shoes should be closed-toe in design and appropriate for the office environment.
    • No visible tattoos or piercings are allowed.
    • English is the mandatory language of communication at our office.
  • **Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action.**
